# Security and Compliance

## Encryption

Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days. Enterprise customers can bring their own keys (BYOK).

## Certifications

Northwind Cloud is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Audit reports are available under NDA on request.

## Access control

SAML single sign-on is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans. Multi-factor authentication is available on all plans. Role-based access control lets owners limit what each member can see.

## Audit logs

Audit logs are retained for 90 days on the Pro plan and for 1 year on the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan does not include audit logs.

## Data residency

Customer data can be stored in the US or the EU region. The region is selected when the workspace is created and cannot be changed afterwards.
