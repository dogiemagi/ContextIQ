# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitments

The Pro plan commits to 99.9% monthly uptime. The Enterprise plan commits to 99.99% monthly uptime. The Starter plan has no uptime SLA, although it runs on the same infrastructure.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment but stays at or above 99.0%, customers receive a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident and are applied to the next invoice.

## Support response times

For priority 1 incidents the first response times are: Starter 48 hours, Pro 8 hours and Enterprise 1 hour. Enterprise customers also receive 24/7 phone support.
