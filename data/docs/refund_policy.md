# Refund Policy

## Annual plans

Customers on annual plans can request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date. After 30 days refunds are not available, but you can cancel renewal at any time from the billing page.

## Monthly plans

Customers on monthly plans can request a refund within 7 days of the first payment. Later monthly payments are not refundable.

## Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days and returned to the original payment method. To request a refund, open a billing ticket or email billing support with your workspace name.

## Exclusions

Usage overage charges, such as extra storage fees, are not refundable. Enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed agreement instead of this policy.
