# Northwind Cloud Pricing

## Plans

### Starter plan
The Starter plan costs $29 per month. It includes 5 seats and 10 GB of storage. Starter is intended for small teams that are getting started.

### Pro plan
The Pro plan costs $99 per month. It includes 25 seats and 200 GB of storage. Pro adds SAML single sign-on, priority support and the 99.9% uptime SLA.

### Enterprise plan
Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated per contract. It includes unlimited seats, a 99.99% uptime SLA, a dedicated support engineer and a choice of data residency region.

## Billing

Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared to paying month by month. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle. Accepted payment methods are credit card and ACH bank transfer. Taxes are added at checkout where applicable.

## Usage overage

Storage beyond the plan limit is billed at $0.10 per GB per month. Overage is invoiced together with the next regular invoice.
